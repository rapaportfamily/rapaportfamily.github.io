Bernard Dov Rapaport was born in Radomysl, Poland, 1888 to Moshe and Menukha. He was a merchant. Prior to
WWII he lived in Tarnów, Poland. During the war he was in Przemyśl, Poland.
Bernard Dov was murdered in the Shoah. Place of death: Auschwitz, Poland, 1940. ₪
