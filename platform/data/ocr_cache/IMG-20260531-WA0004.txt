Regina Rivka Rapaport @ CUTE Em
Regina Rivka Rapaport was born in Nadworna, Poland, 1892 to Leizer and Sara. She was a household. Prior to
WWII she lived in Przemyśl, Poland. During the war she was in Przemyśl, Poland.
Regina Rivka was murdered in the Shoah. Place of death: unknown, 1943. ₪
