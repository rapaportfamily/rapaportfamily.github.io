# The four this archive is about — and the generation before them

## David (Memek) Rapaport

Hebrew name: דוד רפפורט

- Born: 1911-12-25 pl_nadworna
- Died: 1990-08-29 pl_haifa
- Portrait: https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_david_wedding_1938.jpg

Paternal grandfather of Doron, Dana and Daniel. Father of Dov. Holocaust survivor who escaped Nazi-occupied Galicia, reached Brussels in April 1946, and eventually settled in Israel. Forestry engineer, polyglot, member of Betar (revisionist Zionist youth movement).

**profession** — Forestry engineer

**languages** — Polish (native); Italian, German (fluent); Czech, Russian

**movement** — Member of Betar (Poland)

**escape_wagon** — Hid in a niche between cut wooden logs in a railway wagon to escape the ghetto liquidation

**1945_destination_preference** — He and Shimon sailed first in April 1947 because the clandestine immigration would not take mothers with infants, and Dov was months old — not because he and Lusia disagreed about where to go. Memoir p.63. He was a Betar member, which is family memory and may well have shaped the choice of destination, but the SEPARATION had a stated practical cause.

**1938_residence** — Lwów, Poland (1 Jan 1938)

**1942** — Stripped of Polish citizenship

**1946** — Registered as Displaced Person in Brussels (Belgium), 9 April 1946

**post_war** — Wood expert at Israeli customs authority

**escape** — Escaped Nadwórna ghetto in a railway wagon hidden in a niche carved between wooden logs, reached Lwów

**1941_lwow** — Worked on railway/road construction in Lwów under fake identity

**kohanic_lineage** — Kohen (priestly descent) — member of the Rapoport-Kohen rabbinical dynasty from R. Meshullam Yekutiel ha-Kohen Rapa (d.1450, Mainz → Porto). Confirmed family tradition.

**burial** — Sde Yehoshua cemetery (Kfar Samir), Haifa — Section ו, Row 22, Grave 102ד

**haifa_ck_record_id** — 43981 (file 65804)

**lived_to_age** — 78 years (1911-12-25 → 1990-08-29)

**signature_found** — David personally filed the 1953 Yad Vashem Pages of Testimony for his parents (Bernard Dov + Regina Rivka). Dana confirmed the handwriting (31 May 2026) and Dalia recognised the signature (4 Jun 2026) — this is David's own hand. Resolves the long-open hypothesis H4 (locate David's signature).

**family_przemysl_address** — On the Yad Vashem PoT David gives the family's Przemyśl address as ul. Słowackiego 40 (number uncertain — no. 40 was a poultry slaughterhouse; possibly 10 or 64).

**filed_yad_vashem_pages** — On 26 April 1953, from Haifa, David filed the Pages of Testimony at Yad Vashem for three people — No. 53072 for his father, No. 53074 for his mother and No. 53075 for his sister Lotte — signing the relationship line as their son, their son, and her brother. No. 53073 is missing from the run, so there may be a fourth. He gave his father’s year of death as 1940 and his mother’s as 1943; the camp register gives his father 29 March 1942. He was writing from memory, seven years after reaching the country.

## Leah Lusha Rapaport (née Weitzner)

Hebrew name: לאה (לוסיה) רפפורט (לבית וייצנר)

- Born: 1913-04-08 pl_bolechow
- Died: 1996-12-28 pl_haifa
- Portrait: https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_lajka_on_horse.jpg

Paternal grandmother of Doron, Dana and Daniel. Mother of Dov. Holocaust survivor who escaped the Nazi persecution of Galician Jewry by living under multiple false identities (including 'Maria Cizlik'), managing an antiques store in Lwów on the Aryan side. Wrote a memoir of the war.

**nickname_lajka** — Her nickname was Lajka. The memoir, page 9, says her father called her wild — “like a demon”, “like a naughty boy” — but Lajka was what she was called, and she never knew what it meant. The one pre-war photograph of her carries that name as its caption: “Lajka on the horse”.

**marriage** — Married David Rapaport on 10 February 1935 in Bolechów, per the civil marriage record. Her memoir places the wedding in Muszyna in February 1938 — three years later and in another town; the month is right in both. Conflict recorded, not resolved.

**marriage_age_gap** — She was 21 and he was 23 at the marriage of February 1935. The memoir says 18 and 24, which follows from its own later date.

**fake_identity** — Lived in Lwów under fake identity 'Maria Cizlik' (borrowed)

**lwow_residence** — Legionów Street 24, Lwów (with Hormek fake documents) — opposite the theater

**lwow_job** — Manager of art-effects/antiques store under fake identity, ~1941

**przemysl_job** — Briefly worked as teller / in the passport section in Przemyśl, 1939, during the Soviet occupation

**1945_destination_affidavit** — Received an affidavit to emigrate to the USA (per Doron 17 May)

**1945_46_polish_records** — Appears in Polish post-war records 1945-46; not yet located in Brussels 1946 DP records under any spelling. Likely traveled separately from David before they reunited in Brussels for Dov's birth.

**muszyna_job** — Managed a hotel in Muszyna (the resort town) where 'all the social cream' of the town's visitors stayed

**1936_lwow** — In 1936 Lusia moved to Lwow to live with well-off relatives who owned a pharmacy. The purpose of the move was to learn a trade: she was enrolled at a professional dressmaking school, “Kleftinowa”, one of the prestigious schools in the city. Memoir p.15. This replaces an earlier garbled entry that read “attended Carpathian forestry school filings” — which the memoir does not say and which was not traceable to any source.

**memoir** — Wrote memoir of the war (Hebrew)

**burial** — Sde Yehoshua cemetery (Kfar Samir), Haifa — Section ו, Row 22, Grave 102ג (adjacent to David)

**haifa_ck_record_id** — 144046 (file 8011260)

**lived_to_age** — 83 years (1913-04-08 → 1996-12-28)

**years_married_to_david** — ~55-60 years (married ~1934-36 Muszyna → David d.1990)

**refused_america_1947** — She held an affidavit for the United States, sent by her husband’s relative David Grifel, and stood second in the American quota. Her father, already in Palestine, urged her to take it. She refused rather than be separated again from David and Shimon. Memoir p.64.

Lusia was registered the same day at the same committee (card Nr 151738) — placing mother and son together in Katowice, Poland, in mid-1946.

**marriage** — Married Tamar Bugin; two children Ronen (older son) and Hadas (younger sister)

**profession** — Senior Northern-region correspondent at Ma'ariv (מעריב) newspaper for many years. Covered security/defense events, industry, society and regional economics.

**maariv_1990** — Ma'ariv archives, 30 August 1990 (page 81, article 5) — National Library of Israel newspaper search returns this entry for 'שמעון רפפורט'. # The generation before

## Berisz / Bernard / Dov Rapaport

Hebrew name: בעריש / ברנרד / דב רפפורט

- Born: 1886-07-30 pl_radomysl_wielki
- Died: 1942-03-29 pl_auschwitz
- Portrait: https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/documents/basia_2026_08/berish_rapaport_portrait_1924.jpg

Paternal great-grandfather of Doron, Dana and Daniel. Born 30 July 1886 in Radomyśl Wielki (small town between Mielec and Tarnów, in Lesser Poland). Father: Moses (Moshe Saul) Rapaport, merchant. Mother: Menukha (per Yad Vashem Page of Testimony filed 1953). Polish citizen of Jewish faith; industrialist (przemyślowiec) residing in Stanisławów since 1918. Address: Kościuszki 4, Stanisławów. ID card #L/9376/22 issued Stanisławów 16 November 1922; 1924 passport application to Vienna for business; 1927 ID card renewal (USHMM RG-31.064M, family copy via Basia 25 May 2026). Married Rebecca (Regina Rivka) Griffel civilly in Nadwórna on 21 December 1911, four days before son David Mendel's birth. Children: David Mendel (b.25 Dec 1911 Nadwórna) + Lota (b. ~1915). By 1939 living in Przemyśl with Rebecca. When war broke out, Lusia and Shimon traveled there to join them; David followed. Under Soviet rule (Sept 1939 – June 1941). After June 1941 German takeover, per Lusia's memoir page 24, 'several months later' the Germans killed one of David's parents in Przemyśl on a fabricated bomb-on-railway accusation — Hebrew grammar ambiguous between father-in-law and mother-in-law; Basia (Polish reader) interprets it as Rebecca being killed in Przemyśl on the railway-bomb accusation. Berisz himself appears in the Auschwitz Museum victim database (record #188161, transport #689) — so he was deported to Auschwitz and murdered there. Yad Vashem Page of Testimony filed 1953 lists year of death 1940; the Auschwitz Museum record may give a more precise date.

**occupation** — Industrialist (przemyślowiec) per 1924 passport application; merchant (kupiec) per Yad Vashem Page of Testimony. Family economic base appears to have been connected via marriage to the Griffel timber-and-oil industrial empire of Nadwórna.

**stanislawow_resident_since_1918** — Resided in Stanisławów since 1918 per his own 1924 passport application. Address: Kościuszki 4, Stanisławów. The pre-1918 residence and the reason for the move to Stanisławów are not yet documented.

**1911_civil_marriage** — Civilly married Rebecca Griffel in Nadwórna on 21 December 1911, four days before son David Mendel's birth (25 Dec 1911). The 1890-1914 Nadwórna marriage register was lost in WWI and only partially recreated 1932-1935 from secondary documents.

**yad_vashem_pot** — Yad Vashem Page of Testimony record 90394 filed 1953: Bernard Dov Rapaport, born 1888 Radomyśl, parents Moshe + Menukha, merchant, pre-war Tarnów, war Przemyśl, killed Auschwitz 1940. Birth year on PoT (1888) differs from passport application (1886) — the passport application is the primary document.

**auschwitz_victim_record** — Listed in Auschwitz Museum victim database as record #188161, transport #689. URL: https://victims.auschwitz.org/victims/188161

**dabrowa_commune_caveat** — His 1924 passport application lists his commune of belonging as Dąbrowa Tarnowska (Ms. Kasia's reading), not Nadwórna — an unexplained subtlety that may bear on the 'two Moses Sauls' question. By 1939 the family lived in Przemyśl (ul. Słowackiego ~40).

**auschwitz_prisoner_number** — Prisoner number 27310. Arrived at Auschwitz 27 March 1942, died there 29 March 1942 — two days. Registered as robotnik (labourer). Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum victim page.

**auschwitz_transport_route** — Deported on transport 689 — 55 men from the PRISON IN TARNÓW, registered 27 March 1942 with numbers 27305–27359. Berish was 27310. Not deported from Przemyśl directly: he was held in Tarnów, the town his own family came from, and sent by the Sicherheitspolizei of the Kraków district. The Museum records that no documentation survives explaining the circumstances of the transport’s arrival and admission. Eighteen of the fifty-five were identified in the camp records as Jewish, and seventeen of those died at Auschwitz; at least forty-one of the fifty-five died there.

## Rebeka / Regina Rapaport (née Griffel)

Hebrew name: רבקה / רגינה רפפורט (לבית גריפל)

- Born: 1888 pl_nadworna
- Died: 1942 pl_przemysl

Paternal great-grandmother of Doron, Dana and Daniel. Mother of David Mendel and Lota. Born 1888 in Nadwórna, daughter of Eliezer "Zeida" Griffel and Sara Matel Chajes. Yad Vashem Page of Testimony filed 1953 by family lists year of birth as 1892 — likely a memory error by the submitter; the Griffel-family pedigree confirms 1888. Civilly married Berisz Rapaport in Nadwórna on 21 December 1911, four days before son David Mendel was born. The 1890-1914 Nadwórna marriage register was lost in WWI; only a notation on David's birth certificate confirms the marriage. Edward Gelles's "Griffel of Nadworna" pedigree records a religious marriage at Olchowce, województwo lwowskie in 1908 — likely an earlier ritual wedding before civil registration. Pre-war residence: Przemyśl. War: Przemyśl. Per Lusia's memoir page 24 (Basia's Polish reading), killed by Germans in Przemyśl, accused of allegedly placing a bomb on the railway near where the family lived — fabricated charge, likely 1942. Yad Vashem PoT lists 1943 as year of death, place unknown.

**1912_krynica** — Vacationed in Krynica (resort) in summer 1912 — registered at Alfredówka on 25 July 1912, listed as wife of a Nadwórna merchant. Newspaper record.

**gelles_pedigree** — Documented as entry #8 of the 10 children of Eliezer (Zeida) Griffel + Sarah Matel Chajes in Edward Gelles, "Griffel of Nadworna".

**1938_nadworna_property** — Co-owner of a one-storey brick rental house (Dom czynszowy murowany, jednopiętrowy z poddaszem) at Nadwórna ul. Śródmieście. Listed as 'Rebeka z Griffelów zam. Rapaport' (Rebeka née Griffel, married name Rapaport) in the 13 September 1938 auction notice published by Akcyjny Bank Hipoteczny (Lwów). Property auction valued at 127,294 zł, bid deposit 4,500 zł, auction date 28 November 1938. Co-owners: all 17 surviving heirs of Eliezer Griffel + Chaya Griffel-Grünfeld.

## Lota (Lotte) Schmid-Rapaport

Hebrew name: לוטה שמיד־רפפורט

- Born: 1915 pl_vienna
- Died: unknown Lwów

David's sister. Lived in the Lwów ghetto with her family. Per memoir p. 37: bought forged US emigration documents but was reportedly betrayed by the sellers; later disappeared in the war (presumed murdered in the Holocaust). The family memory of her was reconfirmed by Dalia 17 May 2026. — Documented in the JewishGen / Ancestry 'Ten Jewish Ghettos 1939-1942' database under 'Lotte Rapaport, b.1915, Krakow ghetto, 1940' (Reel 13, List 177, Number 72).

**residence** — Lived in Lwów (ghetto from 1941)

**forged_us_documents** — Bought a forged US-residency certificate for $10,000 in the Lwów ghetto 1942-43 (a known scam pattern — see Hotel Polski and similar schemes). The sellers were either Gestapo agents from the outset OR free-lance profiteers who turned in their customers. Lota and her husband were betrayed and arrested. Per memoir p. 41.

**fate** — BETRAYED AND ARRESTED. Per memoir Chapter D page 41: Lota bought a forged document falsely certifying her as a US resident ($10,000 per document — a common Lwów ghetto rescue-scam scheme 1942-43). The sellers informed the Gestapo on the transaction. Lota AND HER HUSBAND (name unrecorded in the memoir) were arrested and taken to a place from which they never returned. The forged document was the very thing that got them killed — no copy survives, as the Gestapo would have confiscated it as evidence. NO Page of Testimony was ever filed for her at Yad Vashem, USHMM, JewishGen, or the Bełżec memorial. The Lusia memoir is currently the only documentary trace of her existence and fate anywhere on Earth. This archive is her memorial.

**existence_confirmed_by_dalia** — Dalia confirmed Lota's existence 17 May 2026 WhatsApp

**kohanic_lineage** — Kohen (priestly descent) — member of the Rapoport-Kohen rabbinical dynasty from R. Meshullam Yekutiel ha-Kohen Rapa (d.1450, Mainz → Porto). Confirmed family tradition.

**hid_shimon_in_lwow_ghetto** — Sheltered her nephew Shimon Rapaport (b.1937) inside the Lwów ghetto for a period 1941-43 before the Hormak family smuggled him out to be reunited with his mother Lusia. Per memoir p. 38, Lota refused Lusia's offer of shelter outside the ghetto: 'I look very Jewish, hide in the basement all the time and eventually you will be caught because of me.'

**husband_identity_unknown** — Lota was married. Per memoir p. 41 she and HER HUSBAND were arrested together after buying the forged US-resident document. The husband's name is not recorded in the memoir — an open research thread. Whoever he was, he became a Rapaport-by-marriage and disappeared with her into the Lwów ghetto destruction 1942-43.

**krakow_ghetto_record_1940** — 🎯 NEW LEAD (Dana, 25 May 2026): Listed as 'Lotte Rapaport', birth date 1915, in the JewishGen 'East Europe, Registers and Listings from Ten Jewish Ghettos, 1939-1942' database via Ancestry.com — Krakow ghetto, document date 1940 (Reel 13, List 177, Number 72). Birth year 1915 matches our Lota exactly (b. ~1915-1916 per 1924 passport: 'Lottr age 8'). Identification highly likely — same first name, same surname, same birth year, in the right geographical area at the right time. Earliest documented evidence of Lota under Nazi rule. Predates the betrayal/arrest episode described in Lusia's memoir (Lwów, ~1942-43). Suggests Lota may have moved from Lwów to Kraków at some point pre-1940 — or that she was in Kraków at war's start and only later moved to Lwów. Full record details (parents, spouse, address) can be retrieved by Doron via the Ancestry source citation.

**husband_name_szmid** — Her husband's surname was SZMID — recorded by David in his Yad Vashem testimony (Basia, 4 Jun 2026). (Earlier the husband was 'name unrecorded' in Lusia's memoir.) Marriage place still unknown. Confirmed at source 11 August 2026: the page is Yad Vashem No. 53075, and it gives her family name as שמיד - רפפורט. Basia’s own tree files her as LOTTA_Rapaport_Szmid_1915 with the husband recorded only as "… Szmid"; Jacob’s tree has no marriage for her at all. Two records name the surname, none names the man.

**lados_verification_pending** — Basia reached Monika Maniewska (co-author of the Ładoś List / passportyzycia.pl) to verify whether Lota was a victim of the forged-passport blackmail or had a genuine escape chance. Call pending as of 15 Jun 2026.

**lwow_corroborated_date_unknown** — WHERE is corroborated; WHEN is not known by anyone. Her brother wrote "Lwów" as the place of death on her Yad Vashem page in 1953. Lusia’s memoir, written independently and decades later, puts her in the Lwów ghetto, has her buy a forged US-residency certificate there, and has her arrested with her husband and never seen again. Two sources that could not have copied one another put her end in the same city. But no source anywhere — not the page, not the memoir, not Basia’s tree, not Jacob’s, both of which record no death for her at all — gives a year. David left that box empty.

**not_her_cousin_charlotte** — DO NOT MERGE with Charlotte Horowitz, born 21 November 1914 in Vienna — the daughter of Jente Rapaport, Berish’s sister, and therefore Lotte’s first cousin. Same name, same city, one year apart, and Basia holds a file placing the cousin in Liège. The Yad Vashem page says 1914, which matches the cousin rather than our Lotte’s 1915 — but the page names her father as בעריש and her mother as רבקה, and those are ours. The year is David writing from memory, off by one, as his father’s year was off by two.
