## The memoir plates (15) — printed inside Lusia’s own book

- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_lajka_on_horse.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): ״לייקה״ על הסוס  
  Translation: “Lajka” on the horse  
  Note: The earliest photograph of Lusia the archive holds, and the only one from before the war. Two paragraphs above it the book explains the name: her father called her wild, “like a naughty boy”, but Lajka was what she was called — and she writes that she never knew what it meant. Bolechów.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_david_wedding_1938.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): מזל טוב! לוסיה וממק ביום חתונתם  
  Translation: Mazal tov! Lusia and Memek on their wedding day  
  Note: One of only two photographs of David known to exist. The memoir places the wedding in February 1938 on a day of ceaseless snow — it calls it a “white wedding” because the snow covered everything in pure white. The civil marriage record says 10 February 1935 in Bolechów. The month agrees; the year and the town do not. The archive records the conflict and does not settle it.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/shimon_baby.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): שמעון התינוק  
  Translation: Shimon the baby  
  Note: Shimon was born in 1937. The chapter this sits in is called “The first signs of antisemitism”, and it opens with Lusia wheeling him in his pram beside the river at Muszyna.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/shimon_family_dog.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): שמעון ליד הכלב המשפחתי  
  Translation: Shimon beside the family dog  
  Note: The lines directly above this photograph record what became of the dog: one day it bit the Germans’ cook, and was shot dead on the spot.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/shimon_holding_baby_dov.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): שמעון ״הגדול״ מחזיק בידיו את אחיו התינוק, דב  
  Translation: Shimon “the big one” holding his baby brother, Dov, in his arms  
  Note: Brussels. The memoir puts Dov’s birth in 1947; the records give 28 August 1946, and the records are right — so Shimon was nine here, not eight. Two and a half years of his own childhood had been spent hidden in a crate.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/shimon_brussels_school.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): שמעון על ספסל בית-הספר בבריסל  
  Translation: Shimon on the school bench in Brussels  
  Note: Brussels, about 1946-47. He was nine or ten. Two and a half years of his childhood had been spent hidden in a crate, and here he is at a school desk.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/shimon_atlit_release_1947.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): תעודת השחרור של שמעון ממחנה המעצר בעתלית  
  Translation: Shimon’s release certificate from the Atlit detention camp  
  Note: A dated document and a photograph of him at once. Haifa, 22 August 1947, signed by the Director of the Immigration Office: “This is to certify that RAPAPORT SH… whose photograph is hereby attached, has been released from ATHLIT Camp… on authority of the Palestine Government. He/she will be registered as an immigrant to Palestine in due course.” He was ten years old, and had been interned first at Karaolos on Cyprus and then at Atlit.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/shimon_dov_cousin_akiva.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): הפגישה, הפגישה שכזאת: האחים שמעון (משמאל) ודוביק (מימין) עם בן־דודם עקיבא אלדר  
  Translation: The reunion, what a reunion: the brothers Shimon (left) and Dovik (right) with their cousin Akiva Eldar  
  Note: The brothers meeting again. David and Shimon had gone ahead and been interned; Lusia and Dov followed on the ship Kedma, landing at Haifa. The caption names Akiva as their cousin. The archive records an Akiva born to Lusia’s sister Feige and Israel Englard, which would make him a first cousin — but that the surname Eldar belongs to the same man is not established.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_with_her_father.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): לוסיה עם אביה בישראל  
  Translation: Lusia with her father, in Israel  
  Note: Elias Weitzner had emigrated in the 1930s while she stayed behind to finish her studies; they had not seen each other since. The page beside this photograph records her walking down a Haifa street, seeing a policeman in uniform, and going rigid — until her father calmed her: “Here everyone is Jewish. The policemen are Jewish too.”
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/matel_weinreb_portrait.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): אמא של לוסיה  
  Translation: Lusia’s mother  
  Note: Matel Weinreb of Dolina, daughter of the innkeeper Samuel Weinreb. She married Elias Weitzner at Dolina in 1905. This is the only photograph of her the archive holds.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_in_israel.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): לוסיה נראית נינוחה ומאושרת במדינת ישראל  
  Translation: Lusia looks relaxed and happy in the State of Israel  
  Note: The chapter is about the one-room flat on Abbas Street in Haifa — a small kitchen, the toilet outside, 250 steps to climb with the shopping, and no bus that came that way. She writes that she was happy in it.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_purim_costume.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): לוסיה כ״נערה מהוואי״ בנשף פורים שערך בביתה  
  Translation: Lusia as a “Hawaiian girl” at the Purim party held at her home  
  Note: The flat at Moriah Street 93 in Haifa, bought with the German reparations money. They cleared the furniture into the garden; she made the costume herself out of raffia; Memek handled the drinks and the music, and they danced until the small hours.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/rapaport_family_picnic.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): משפחת רפפורט בפיקניק. מימין לשמאל: ממק, שמעון, לוסיה ודוביק  
  Translation: The Rapaport family on a picnic. Right to left: Memek, Shimon, Lusia and Dovik  
  Note: The only photograph in this archive that holds all four of them at once — David, Lusia, Shimon and Dov, together and out of danger. Israel, undated; the surrounding chapter covers Lusia’s eleven years at the post office.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_bnai_brith_delegate_card.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): כרטיס הציר של לוסיה לועידת ״בני־ברית״  
  Translation: Lusia’s delegate card for the B’nai B’rith convention  
  Note: A “delegate certificate” issued by B’nai B’rith in Israel, Galilee Lodge, Jerusalem, naming her as delegate of the Hermon lodge to the forty-fifth annual convention. She ran the lodge’s activity for children aged 6 to 13 — sports equipment for a school on the Carmel, a collective bar mitzvah, and a set at her own home for the neighbourhood.
- https://rapaportfamily.github.io/assets/research_images/memoir/lusia_back_cover_portrait.jpg  
  Caption as printed (Hebrew): לוסיה רפפורט (בתמונה)  
  Translation: Lusia Rapaport (pictured)  
  Note: The back cover of the book. It carries no caption of its own — the blurb beside it names her, “Lusia Rapaport (pictured)”. Undated; the memoir was dictated in 1986. It is the clearest photograph of her the archive holds.
