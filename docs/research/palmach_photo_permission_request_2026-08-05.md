## English version (if preferred)

**To the Palmach Archive, Palmach Generation Association**

**Subject: request for permission to use "Theodor Herzl" photographs in a private family archive**

My name is Doron Rapaport. I am building a private family archive for the eightieth birthday of my
father, **Dov (Bernard) Rapaport**, on 28 August 2026. He was born in Brussels on 28 August 1946 to
Holocaust survivors from Galicia.

My request concerns the ship **Theodor Herzl** directly. My grandfather **David (Memek) Rapaport**,
born Nadwórna 25 December 1911, and my uncle **Shimon Rapaport**, born Lwów 22 June 1937 — then a
boy of nine — sailed on her from **Sète** on **2 April 1947**. She was boarded on 13 April and three
ma'apilim were killed. The two were deported to Cyprus and held at Karaolos. **Shimon's release
certificate from the Atlit detention camp is dated 22 August 1947**, and it is printed in the memoir
my grandmother Lusia Rapaport dictated in 1986.

My grandmother and my father, then an infant, were left behind in Brussels — Aliyah Bet did not
carry mothers with babies — and only reached Israel in 1948, aboard the *Kedma*.

**My request:** in the Palmach photo treasury, gallery *פלי"ם ספינות המעפילים ב*, I found **19
photographs** captioned "תיאודור הרצל (GUARDIAN)", items **46947 to 46965**. I ask your permission to
show some of them in the private family archive I am building for my father.

The archive is **non-commercial** and carries no advertising. I will gladly credit every photograph
"**All rights reserved to the Palmach Generation Association**", or in any wording you specify, and
restrict access to invited family members if you prefer. I am happy to pay any fee required.

**One further question:** do you, or the records of Ha'Mossad Le'Aliya Bet, hold a **passenger list**
for the sailing of 2 April 1947? Such a document might carry the names of David and Shimon Rapaport,
and for our family that would be a greater find than the photographs.

The birthday is 28 August 2026, so any reply you can give before then would be deeply appreciated.

With thanks and with great respect for your work,

Doron Rapaport
