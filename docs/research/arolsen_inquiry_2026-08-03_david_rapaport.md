## 2. What we already hold, and where it comes from

Each of these is a document in our possession; copies can be supplied on request.

- **Birth**: Nadwórna civil birth register — 25 December 1911, son of Berisz Rapaport and
  Rebeka Griffel.
- **Marriage**: civil marriage record, Bolechów, **10 February 1935**, to Lea Weitzner
  (b. 8 April, Bolechów — the year appears as 1913 in the civil birth register and as 1916 on
  three later records, and is unresolved).
- **Wartime, Nadwórna and Lwów**: family testimony records his escape from the Nadwórna ghetto,
  a work permit for the *Foresta* timber operation in Nadwórna, and survival in Lwów under false
  identity. No document corroborates these.
- **Citizenship**: recorded as stripped of Polish citizenship in **1942**.
- **Displaced Person, Brussels**: DIPIS card, registered **9 April 1946**.
  ⚠ **Note for your searchers:** this card gives his place of birth as **"Cieszyn (Tesin)"**.
  That is a transcription error by the registering clerk — the Nadwórna birth register is
  decisive. Records indexed under *Cieszyn* may therefore be his.
- **Wife and son, post-war Poland**: Central Committee of Polish Jews (CKŻP) survivor cards
  filed at the **Katowice** committee on **1 July 1946** — Szymon, card **nr 151337**; Lusia,
  card **nr 151738**. Both give last known address Katowice.
- **April 1947**: sailed from Sète, France aboard the *Theodor Herzl*; the ship was boarded by
  the Royal Navy on 13 April 1947 and the passengers deported to Cyprus. David and his son
  Szymon were interned at **Karaolos, Famagusta**. Family testimony; no document held.

## 3. What we are asking for

Any document in your holdings — digitised or not — concerning:

1. **David / Dawid Rapaport**, born 25 December 1911 in Nadwórna. In particular any
   **CM/1 application**, **IRO or UNRRA file**, **DP registration or camp record from Belgium**,
   or any **ITS correspondence file** opened in his name. His Brussels DP registration of April
   1946 suggests such a file should exist.
2. His wife **Lea / Lusia Rapaport née Weitzner** and son **Szymon Rapaport** (b. 22 June 1937
   Lwów), who were registered at Katowice in July 1946 and travelled to Belgium thereafter.
3. His parents, both murdered in the Shoah and for whom we have no death record:
   - **Berisz (Bernard) Rapaport**, born 30 July 1886 in Radomyśl Wielki. Our research indicates
     he died at **Auschwitz** and associates him with victim number **188161** — we would be
     grateful if you could confirm or correct this from your own records.
   - **Rebeka (Regina) Rapaport née Griffel**, born 1888 in Nadwórna.
4. Any record under the **variant spellings** Rappaport, Rapoport, Rappoport, and under the
   erroneous birthplace **Cieszyn / Tesin** noted above.

## 4. Why we are asking

This is a family archive assembled by David's grandchildren. It is being completed for his son
**Dov Rapaport's 80th birthday on 28 August 2026** — Dov was born in Brussels in 1946, while his
father was interned in Cyprus, and he has never seen a document from those years beyond the DP
card. No Page of Testimony was ever filed at Yad Vashem for Berisz or Rebeka; this archive is at
present their only written memorial.
