# Rapaport Family Research — State of Knowledge as of 1 June 2026

This document summarises what we know, how we know it, and what earlier conclusions we have had to retract. It is a working brief for Dalia, Dana, Daniel and Doron Rapaport — and for Basia, Magda, Kasia and Ms. Kasia.

Drafted by Claude Code (CC) on 1 June 2026 after the third WhatsApp-export ingest of the family-tree project. Every claim below is sourced; speculation is marked.

## 6. The three highest-value open questions

1. **Lota's full ghetto record.** The Ancestry "Ten Ghettos 1939-1942" reference (Reel 13, List 177, Entry 72) may include parents' names, spouse's name, address. Pulling the full record could finally identify Lota's husband.
2. **Berisz's first wife (if any).** The 1924 passport lists him as having only Mendel (12) and Lottr (8). No earlier marriage on record. But Edward Gelles dates the religious marriage to 1908 Olchowce, civil to 1911 Nadwórna — that 3-year gap is unusual. Worth searching Stanisławów records pre-1908.
3. **The Tarnów Moses Saul Rapaport headstones.** Two stones in the preserved Tarnów cemetery: one died 11 Aug 1933; the other 30 Oct 1931 from Dąbrowa, 7th-generation descendant of Shabtai HaKohen ("the Shach"). Either could be our Moses Saul. Resolving this would tie our line to a specific rabbinical descent.
