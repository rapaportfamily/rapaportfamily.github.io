## B. Jente Rapaport SURVIVED — and her line runs through Liège, Belgium

Previously Jente was a great-grand-aunt with a documented 1920 second marriage and an undocumented fate. The chat proves she **survived the war**:

- Jente's husband is now read as **Meschulim (Meshulam) Isacher Horowitz of Sokal** (the 1920 Tarnów record had been read "Mendel Eachem"; the Liège record gives "Meschilin/Meschulim Isacher"). They had a daughter:

- **Liège marriage record No. 1142 / II42**: on **15 June 1946** Szewa married **Markus Türkel**, a British-Army soldier residing in Tel Aviv, son of **Leib Türkel + Lea Nüssenbaum**. The record states the bride's mother **"Jenta Rapaport … living together with her daughter"** → **Jente was alive in Liège in 1946.** (Dana's translation 8 Jun 22:44; Basia's confirmed EN translation 10 Jun 15:48.)
- Markus + Szewa are **buried in Holon Cemetery (Bat Yam, Tel Aviv District)**; Szewa **died 1985**. (Basia 10 Jun 15:39; Dana 8 Jun 23:40.)

**Double Turkel–Horowitz tie**: Alte Leja married a Turkel; her line and Jente's line married into each other again via Markus Türkel × Szewa Horowitz. Cross-witnessing supports it — the birth record of Alte Leja's first child (Szeindla Jutta, 1907) carries a legitimation note by **Jechiel Turkel** with **Meschulem Horowitz signing as witness** (Basia, 8 Jun 20:11).
